运营方与适用范围

本应用由临沂市丰境电子商务有限责任公司（以下简称“我们”）运营，供公司内部使用。本声明说明您授权本应用连接亚马逊广告账户时，我们如何处理与该授权相关的数据。本声明不替代亚马逊自身的隐私政策。

我们处理哪些数据

在获得账户授权后，我们可能获取和保存广告账户及 Profile 标识、广告活动和广告组信息、广告与投放设置，以及曝光、点击、广告花费、归因销售额等广告效果报告数据。为了维持授权连接，我们还会处理应用凭据和授权令牌。我们不通过这项广告集成主动请求买家的姓名、地址或支付卡信息。

数据用途

上述数据用于连接获授权的亚马逊广告账户、同步和展示广告报告、开展内部分析与对账，以及维护连接的安全和正常运行。我们不会出售通过该集成取得的数据。

访问、存储与共享

数据保存在我们使用的 ERPNext 系统中，仅向因工作需要而获得权限的人员开放。为了运行服务，相关技术服务提供方可能按我们的安排处理必要数据；在法律要求或取得相应授权时，我们也可能披露必要信息。我们不会为无关的广告营销目的向第三方提供这些数据。

保留、撤销授权与删除

我们在实现上述用途所需的期间内保留数据；财务、审计或法律义务可能要求保留部分记录。您可以通过亚马逊账户的授权管理撤销本应用的访问权限。撤销后，我们将停止通过该授权获取新数据。您也可以通过下方邮箱提出访问、更正或删除请求；我们会在核实请求后，按适用要求处理。

Fengjing Amazon Ads Privacy Notice

For Fengjing Amazon Ads Reporting

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Operator and scope

This internal application is operated by Linyi Fengjing E-commerce Co., Ltd. (“we” or “us”). This notice explains how we process data associated with an authorization to connect an Amazon Ads account. It does not replace Amazon's own privacy policy.

Data we process

After an account is authorized, we may retrieve and store advertising account and profile identifiers, campaign and ad group information, ad and targeting settings, and performance reports such as impressions, clicks, spend, and attributed sales. We also process application credentials and authorization tokens to maintain the connection. This Ads integration does not intentionally request buyers' names, addresses, or payment card details.

How we use the data

We use this data to connect authorized Amazon Ads accounts, synchronize and display advertising reports, perform internal analysis and reconciliation, and maintain the security and operation of the connection. We do not sell data obtained through this integration.

Access, storage, and sharing

Data is stored in the ERPNext system we use and is accessible only to personnel with a work-related need and appropriate permissions. Technical service providers may process necessary data on our behalf to operate the service. We may also disclose information when required by law or with appropriate authorization. We do not provide this data to third parties for unrelated advertising purposes.

Retention, revocation, and deletion

We retain data for as long as needed for the purposes above. Financial, audit, or legal obligations may require us to keep some records longer. You can revoke this application's access through your Amazon account's authorization settings; we will then stop obtaining new data under that authorization. You may also contact us to request access, correction, or deletion. We will review and handle such requests after verification, subject to applicable requirements.

Contact

For questions about this notice or our handling of advertising data, email 1060778506@qq.com.

Changes to this notice

If our data practices change, we will update this page and the date above.
